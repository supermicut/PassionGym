Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

No account. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress is saved on your device.

Google Firebase collects anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can fix problems and improve the game.

Yodo1 MAS and its ad partners serve optional rewarded videos and use device identifiers to do so. You can limit ad personalisation at any time.

Passion Gym (Android package com.AlexandruSimion.PassionGym) is developed and published by Alexandru Simion, an independent developer based in Romania ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. This policy covers the Passion Gym app and this website.

What we collect

Data stored on your device

Your levels, stars and hearts, coins, gems, unlocked outfits and episodes, daily goal progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Google Firebase

Firebase is a service of Google LLC (in the EEA and UK, Google Ireland Limited). We use:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase to see, in aggregate, how players use the game: which features are used, where players get stuck, how long sessions last and which devices are common. Analytics uses an app instance ID and, where permitted, the Android advertising ID.
  • Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, which contain the stack trace, device model, OS version, available memory and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Google keeps this crash data for 90 days.

Google processes this data under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

Rewarded videos (Yodo1 MAS)

The game shows only optional rewarded videos, for example to double your coins after a session or to get extra moves or energy. There are no banner ads. You choose when to watch one.

Ads are delivered through the Yodo1 Managed Ad Service (MAS), provided by Yodo1 Ltd. MAS is a mediation platform: when you ask for a video, it requests ads from several ad networks and shows the best available one. Depending on your region, these networks can include Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Mintegral, Pangle, Liftoff (Vungle), InMobi and Meta Audience Network. Yodo1 and these partners act as independent controllers of the data they collect and use it to serve, cap, measure and verify ads and, where you allow it, to personalise ads based on your interests.

The game uses the consent and age tools built into Yodo1 MAS. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), you are asked for consent before your data is used for personalised advertising. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalised.

To learn more, see the privacy information published by Yodo1, Google (AdMob), AppLovin and Unity.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Consent for personalised advertising and the related use of your advertising ID. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests for crash reporting, aggregate analytics, non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and keeping the game secure. These help us keep a free game working and funded.
  • Performance of a contract to deliver the rewards you choose to earn by watching a video.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID. Once deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead.
  • Ad consent: you can change your choice in the consent dialog shown by the game, or by clearing the app's data, which shows the dialog again on the next launch.
  • Rewarded videos: they are optional. If you never tap a video button, no ad is shown.
  • Everything: uninstalling the game stops all collection from the app.

Retention and transfers

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Firebase Crashlytics data is kept for 90 days. Google Analytics event data is kept for the period set in our Analytics settings, at most 14 months. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

Google, Yodo1 and some ad partners process data outside the EEA, including in the United States and Singapore. These transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to withdraw consent. Because we do not run accounts or servers of our own, we cannot link Firebase or ad data to you by name. To help us find your data, include your Firebase installation ID or advertising ID if you contact us, and we will pass deletion requests to Google and Yodo1. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Romania this is the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP).

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can exercise the opt-out by declining personalised ads in the consent dialog or by deleting your advertising ID. We do not sell personal information for money.

Age limit

Passion Gym is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the game, contact us and we will ask Google and Yodo1 to delete the related data.
